Classic Pancakes

Description

Soft, fluffy, and easy to make, these classic pancakes are perfect for breakfast or brunch. They are served best with maple syrup, butter, or fresh fruits.

This recipe is beginner-friendly and only requires a few simple ingredients that are commonly available at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix all dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and pour the batter.
  5. Cook both sides until golden brown.
  6. Serve hot with syrup or fruits.
