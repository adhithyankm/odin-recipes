Chocolate Milkshake

Description

Chocolate milkshake is a cold and refreshing drink loved by both kids and adults. It is creamy, sweet, and perfect for hot summer days.

This recipe takes only a few minutes to prepare and tastes even better with ice cream on top.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold milk
  • 3 tablespoons chocolate syrup
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a glass.
  4. Top with ice cream or chocolate syrup.
  5. Serve immediately.
