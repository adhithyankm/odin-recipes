Chicken Fried Rice

Description

Chicken fried rice is a quick and flavorful meal made with rice, vegetables, and tender chicken pieces. It is a popular dish enjoyed around the world.

This recipe is perfect for using leftover rice and can be prepared in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup chicken pieces
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped carrot
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Cook chicken until fully done.
  3. Add vegetables and stir fry for 3 minutes.
  4. Push ingredients to the side and scramble eggs.
  5. Add cooked rice and soy sauce.
  6. Mix everything well and serve hot.
